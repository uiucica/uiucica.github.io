1. Scope, Method and Limitations

This report catalogues food retailers in the Champaign-Urbana metropolitan area that stock groceries associated with specific national or regional cuisines, and maps those cuisines to the stores that carry them. Only businesses verified as operating as of 24 July 2026 are included in the directory. Businesses that have closed are listed separately in Section 8 so that stale directory entries elsewhere on the web can be discounted.

Sources. Google Places business listings and customer reviews retrieved 24 July 2026; openings and closings coverage from Smile Politely (Champaign-Urbana's culture magazine); the ChambanaMoms international grocery roundup (last updated July 2025); the University of Illinois McKinley Health Center international grocery guide; and individual store websites.

Limitations, stated plainly. Product attributions are derived from published store descriptions and customer reviews, not from a physical shelf audit. Independent grocers rotate stock heavily and a store described here as carrying a given cuisine will not carry every item from it. Two entries carry explicit status warnings. Trading hours are as published by the operators on 24 July 2026 and change without notice. Anyone travelling for a specific ingredient should telephone first.

3.3 Middle Eastern, Mediterranean and Broad International

World Harvest International & Gourmet Foods 4.4 / 5 (314 reviews)
Address306 W Springfield Ave, Urbana, IL 61801
Telephone(217) 356-4444
HoursMon-Sat 10:00-20:00; Sun 10:00-19:00
Rating4.4 / 5 (314 reviews)

The widest single-store international assortment in Champaign-Urbana, and the default answer for Middle Eastern and Mediterranean ingredients, which no other local store covers in depth. The business describes itself as the area's first and finest international and gourmet food store, with more than ten thousand lines. Strengths are the olive and cheese counters, bulk olive oil dispensed to your own bottle, spices, herbal teas, nuts and dried fruit, and fresh dates arriving each August and September. The store now trades at the former Strawberry Fields site and has retained that business's natural and organic grocery, bakery, made-fresh salads and sandwiches, coffee, supplements and body-care ranges alongside the international goods. A café operates on site.

Cuisines stocked: Lebanon, Syria, Palestine, Jordan, Turkey, Greece, Iran, Egypt and North Africa; India and Pakistan; Southeast Asia; Eastern and Western Europe; Latin America.

Older directories list World Harvest at 519 E University Ave, Champaign. That address is out of date. The store is in Urbana.

6.1 Payment friction at independent stores

These are not membership requirements but they will catch out a first-time visitor:

  • Annapoorna Stores and Maligaya's Oriental Market both apply a reported $10 minimum on card transactions. Carry cash for small purchases.
  • El Progreso and El Progreso Latino operate primarily in Spanish. Basic Spanish helps for special requests at the butcher counter.
  • Fresh International Market does not stock its customer toilet with paper; a reviewer reports being directed to buy it from the shelf.
  • Signature Meats applies preparation charges on fish that at least one customer disputes as inconsistently applied. Agree the total before ordering.

7. Gaps in the Market

Cuisines with a local population or restaurant presence but no dedicated retail source. Listed because knowing what is not available locally is as useful as knowing what is.
